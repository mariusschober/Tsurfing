# Recorded clock audio

Source: “Clock” (sound 0007), Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank.
https://bigsoundbank.com/clock-s0007.html
License: CC0 (public domain), verified 7 September 2026.
https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html

The two bundled WAVs are excerpts of the real clock recording, alternating
tick and tock. Original: 44,100 Hz, 16-bit, mono, 9 seconds.
Excerpts: 2.20–2.70 seconds and 3.20–3.70 seconds.
Processing: remove DC offset, common 0.65 gain, 5 ms entrance / 10 ms exit fades.
No oscillator, synthetic noise, pitch shift, or time stretching is used.
